Extract Parts

Customers rarely receive a clean, single-part file. What lands on the desk is a dirty construction drawing: a quote, offer or assembly DXF with many parts nested on one sheet, tangled up with dimensions, part texts, title blocks and construction lines - and with the same edges often drawn two or three times. Before any of those parts can go to production it has to be lifted out of that clutter and rebuilt as one clean, closed, single-part contour.

Extract Parts is the workflow that does exactly that. You isolate the one part you want, save it to its own file, and then let the Select and Autocleanup tools turn the leftover tangle of loose lines, arcs and spline fragments into a production-ready part: inner and outer contours separated, the outer boundary welded into one closed polyline, bend lines converted so they fold instead of cut, and the stray texts stripped out.

Steps:

  • Select the part - marquee or pick the entities that make up the single part you want to keep.

  • File › Save Selected As…​ - write just that selection to its own file and carry on working in it, leaving the busy source drawing untouched.

  • Explode Polyline (Polygon group) - dissolve the polylines that chain inner and outer contours together, so each contour stands on its own again. Pair it with Select Polylines (Select group) to explode the whole drawing in one step.

  • Create Outer Contour (Autocleanup group) - merge the outer boundary into a single closed polyline; for a specific inner contour, click inside it with Follow Path.

  • Convert the bend lines with the Bend tool - click a line to turn it into a real bend line so the part folds there instead of being cut. (Future: auto-select the bend lines by layer or colour.)

  • Select Text (Select group) and delete - strip the part numbers and annotations.

Experimental features

Experimental features

Pix is growing a set of experimental tools. Each one removes a recurring, time-consuming step when turning real customer DXF/DWG data into a clean, production-ready sheet-metal part. Every section below has a short description, a demo video, and a pointer to the detailed design notes under C:\Work\PixExperimental\MD.

  • Extract Parts — lift a single part out of a multi-part quote or nest: select it, save it, dissolve the polylines shared between inner and outer contours, merge the outer boundary into one closed polyline, convert bend lines and strip the texts.

  • Create Outer Contour — break up circles, polygons and loose or overshooting segments and trace the outer boundary into one closed contour automatically.

  • Follow Path — click inside a contour (for example a letter or an inner cut-out) and connect all of its elements into one closed contour.

  • Overlay Deletion — double-drawn lines make a laser cut twice. Pix marks every overlay in red-brown the moment a file opens and removes them on demand, with a tolerance you can raise to catch sloppier copies.

  • Layers — identify a layer by clicking an element, toggle and lock layers, and auto-remove dimensions and centre-mark crosshairs.

  • Annotate Technology — carry bend and laser technology across the import/export boundary.

  • Perforate — prototype a bend without a press brake by perforating the bend line.

  • Metric / Inch — force a consistent unit conversion when a DXF’s unit metadata is wrong or inconsistent.

  • Small editing conveniences support these workflows too — for example Esc clears the current selection (see C:\Work\PixExperimental\MD\unselectESC.md).

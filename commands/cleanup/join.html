Join

Join

The Join tool combines separate line and arc segments into a single connected polyline. It is the main tool for rebuilding a usable contour from fragmented geometry — for example after importing a drawing whose outline arrived as many disconnected pieces.

Joining segments

  1. Select the Join tool.

  2. Click a segment to start the result polyline.

  3. Click the next segment to add. Pix trims or extends the segments at their intersection as needed and shows a live preview of the joined result.

  4. Continue clicking segments to grow the polyline.

When the joined segments form a closed loop, the result is completed automatically.

While joining:

  • Hold kbd:[Ctrl] and click to finalise the current polyline and start a new one.

  • Hold kbd:[Shift] and click to undo the last segment you added.

The finished polyline is created on the default layer, and the original segments that were joined are consumed.

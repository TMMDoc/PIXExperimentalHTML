Measure

The Measure tool is used to measure distances and angles of entities in a drawing.

Select the Measure tool and then move the mouse over the canvas area to see the X and Y coordinates of the mouse pointer.

Hover over a drawing entity to snap to it and display its distance and angle. The end points of the entity are highlighted with a small red circle.

Linear measurement

For linear measurements, length and angle are displayed.

Arc measurement

For arc measurements, radius and span of the arc are displayed.

Measure by selecting two points

You can also click to set a starting point and then move the mouse to see the distance and angle from that point to the current mouse position.

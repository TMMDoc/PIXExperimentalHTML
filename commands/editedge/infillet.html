Inside fillet

Inside fillet

The Inside Fillet tool is used to create an inside fillet on a corner of a polyline by specifying the fillet radius.

Creating an inside fillet

Type in the fillet radius and click near a corner to create an inside fillet.

The side of the segment you click determines whether the inside fillet will have a major arc or a minor arc.

Creating inside fillets on all corners

You can apply inside fillet to all corners by holding down the Ctrl key when clicking near a corner.

The inside fillet is created only if both the line segments meeting at the corner are straight line segments, and of sufficient length.
